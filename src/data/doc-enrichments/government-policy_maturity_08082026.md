## Saudi-NCA-NCS1-2020
- **Source**: Saudi NCA — National Cryptographic Standards (NCS-1:2020)
- **URL**: https://nca.gov.sa/
- **Requirement count**: 4
- **Governance**:
    - _T2 Risk-Informed · all_: Establish documented policy to select and implement cryptographic strength levels (MODERATE or ADVANCED) based on data sensitivity and system nature.
    - _T2 Risk-Informed · certificates_: Adhere to prescribed algorithms and validity periods for Public Key Infrastructure (PKI) certificates as mandated by the standards.
- **Lifecycle / CLM**:
    - _T2 Risk-Informed · keys_: Implement Key Lifecycle Management (KLM) processes to manage keys from creation through destruction, ensuring standardized usage across processes.
    - _T2 Risk-Informed · keys_: Enforce key protection and lifetime requirements as defined in the national standards to ensure proper key management.
