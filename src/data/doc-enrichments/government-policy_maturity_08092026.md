## QCCPA-2022
- **Source**: Quantum Computing Cybersecurity Preparedness Act (H.R.7535)
- **URL**: https://www.congress.gov/bill/117th-congress/house-bill/7535/text/statute
- **Requirement count**: 4
- **Governance**:
    - _T3 Repeatable · all_: Develop a plan to migrate agency IT to post-quantum cryptography consistent with OMB prioritization guidance.
- **Inventory**:
    - _T3 Repeatable · software_: Establish and maintain a current inventory of IT vulnerable to quantum decryption, prioritized per OMB criteria.
- **Lifecycle / CLM**:
    - _T3 Repeatable · software_: Prioritize IT for migration to post-quantum cryptography within one year of NIST issuing standards.
- **Observability**:
    - _T3 Repeatable · all_: Report inventory and migration progress to OMB, CISA, and National Cyber Director annually and on an ongoing basis.
