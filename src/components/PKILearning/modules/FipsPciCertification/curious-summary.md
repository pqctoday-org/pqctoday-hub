### What This Is About

A closer look at two certificates an HSM meets first: FIPS 140-3, the US and Canadian standard for cryptographic modules, and PCI approval for payment HSMs together with the rules for operating them. It builds on the certification fundamentals module.

### Why It Matters

Passing algorithm tests is only the first step toward FIPS 140-3; the module certificate is a separate result. For payment devices, the device approval and the rules for the companies operating it are different things, and both matter.

### The Key Takeaway

A queue position is not a certificate, and a PCI listing's post-quantum flag only says that support exists — it does not approve a specific algorithm.

### What's Happening

As of 24 September 2026, the FIPS 140-3 standard itself is unchanged, while a US executive order directs NIST to speed up its validation process by about 19 December 2026. PCI's newest HSM standard adds post-quantum definitions without naming a required algorithm or deadline.
