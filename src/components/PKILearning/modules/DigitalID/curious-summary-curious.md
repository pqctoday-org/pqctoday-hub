New digital identity wallets allow citizens to prove who they are using their smartphones. These apps use digital signatures to officially bind a person's identity to their device, acting like a tamper-evident digital passport.

If a future hacker uses a quantum computer to break these signatures, they could perfectly forge someone else's identity, leading to massive, untraceable fraud. Because these identities will be used for everything from banking to voting, it matters that the backbone of these digital ID systems can move to Post-Quantum security; the eIDAS 2.0 and reference-framework texts we checked do not mention quantum computing. The aim is to keep fake digital identities from being created, even by quantum computers.
