### What This Is About

A closer look at the certificates that matter most in Europe: Common Criteria, the EU's EUCC scheme built on it, and the eIDAS rules for qualified signature and seal devices. It builds on the certification fundamentals module.

### Why It Matters

A Common Criteria grade such as "EAL4+" only means something once you know which extra checks were added. In the EU the same product can meet Common Criteria three ways at once, and each affects what a post-quantum change needs.

### The Key Takeaway

"EAL4+" means nothing until its extra components are named, and EUCC is a legal scheme — not a Protection Profile.

### What's Happening

As of 24 September 2026, the EU's scheme already lists post-quantum algorithms in its approved cryptography guidance, and says lattice-based algorithms should be combined with a classical one.
