The massive 5G cellular networks powering millions of smartphones and connected vehicles rely on incredibly complex webs of digital trust between cell towers, devices, and the internet core.

Because a quantum computer could effortlessly shatter the digital locks protecting these high-speed connections, attackers could theoretically instantly reroute or intercept massive amounts of global cellular traffic. The industry is still at the planning stage: groups such as ATIS and GSMA are recommending how operators should prepare, but 3GPP has not yet standardised any post-quantum protection for subscriber identities in 5G.
