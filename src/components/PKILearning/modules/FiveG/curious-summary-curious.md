The massive 5G cellular networks powering millions of smartphones and connected vehicles rely on incredibly complex webs of digital trust between cell towers, devices, and the internet core.

Shor-capable quantum computers would threaten the elliptic-curve key agreement in SUCI Profiles A and B. That could expose the subscriber-specific identifier in a recorded SUCI; it would not decrypt all cellular traffic or automatically reveal a person's identity or movements. The public ETSI document linked here is TS 33.501 Release 19 V19.7.0 (February 2026). 3GPP's specification record lists Release 20 V20.3.0 as a later version. 3GPP also has a Release 20 study (TR 33.703, under change control) and a PCG-approved work item specifically for post-quantum SUCI migration, but neither is an adopted normative post-quantum profile.
