### What This Is About

PQC risk management provides a framework to prepare for the possible arrival of a Cryptographically Relevant Quantum Computer (CRQC). It involves modeling CRQC timelines as scenarios (no one knows when one will exist) and identifying which cryptographic assets (like RSA and ECC) would be exposed.

### Why It Matters

A CRQC could break the asymmetric cryptography that protects most digital communications today. Rating each asset by Likelihood x Impact helps prioritize migration efforts, especially for long-lived secrets exposed to Harvest-Now-Decrypt-Later (HNDL) risk.

### The Key Takeaway

Use the dates that actually apply to you. If you supply U.S. National Security Systems, CNSA 2.0 sets them; otherwise use your regulator's and your customers' dates. The method has three steps: CRQC scenario planning, building a comprehensive risk register, and visualizing exposure on a 5x5 likelihood-impact heatmap to guide executive decision-making. The heatmap ranks your assets against each other; it does not measure when a quantum computer will arrive.

### What's Happening

Organizations are incorporating CRQC scenario planning into their enterprise risk frameworks, using likelihood-impact heatmaps to prioritize which cryptographic systems must migrate first based on HNDL exposure and the deadlines that apply to them.
