Think of a security architect as someone deciding where the locks and vaults belong in a digital building. Making the locks easier to replace helps future changes, but each new lock still needs compatibility and performance checks.

This workshop offers six steps: explore six scenarios, rate your skills, select an action checklist, answer nine exposure questions, try the Strangler Fig migration model and use Trust Hardware Selector. The selector puts eight kinds of trust hardware side by side, explaining where they sit, who controls them and what their cited sources establish. It keeps a proposed design separate from a product or certificate.

Use separate tools for the next questions: the [Cert Capacity Calculator](/playground/cert-capacity) estimates certificate payload and renewal storage, the [TLS Simulator](/playground/tls-simulator) explores sample connection settings, and [Hybrid Certificates](/playground/hybrid-certs) compares sample certificate formats. They are linked resources, not hidden calculators in the scenario cards.

Use these examples to choose what your own tools must check. They do not predict your system's response time, prove that all clients accept a certificate, or demonstrate a completed production migration.
