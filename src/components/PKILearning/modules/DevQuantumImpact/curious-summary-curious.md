A programmer needs to know what changes when an app uses new cryptography. Bigger keys and signatures can put pressure on the app's size limits, and new library calls or protocol settings may need different tests. Whether something breaks depends on the app and its configuration.

This role workshop starts with six expandable examples. You can rate your skills, follow learning links, select an action checklist and answer nine questions about your work. It does not scan your code or secretly run a calculator inside those cards.

For a practical comparison, open the separate [Token Size Analyzer](/learn/api-security-jwt?tab=workshop&step=4). Change the sample message and see how token and Authorization header-line sizes change. The [TLS Simulator](/playground/tls-simulator), [hybrid key example](/playground/hybrid-encrypt) and [test-input lesson (on a computer)](/learn/acvp-lab-workflow?tab=workshop&step=1) show other parts of the job; the test-input lesson runs no engine. The [API migration examples](/learn/crypto-dev-apis?tab=workshop&step=7) and [code-signing sample](/learn/code-signing?tab=workshop&step=0) help you decide which code changes and signing checks to investigate.

Take one decision back to your own tools: which size limit, connection setting or test needs checking? A sample helps you choose the check; it does not prove that your app works or that a product is certified.
