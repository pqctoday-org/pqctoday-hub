### What This Is About

Your organization's quantum timeline is limited by your slowest vendor. Third-party software, hardware, and cloud providers handling sensitive data are massive, immediate targets for "Harvest Now, Decrypt Later" campaigns.

### Why It Matters

A commercial product that does not use CNSA 2.0 algorithms cannot be used to protect National Security Systems unless NSA has provided specific written guidance otherwise, and CNSSP 15 states that by 1 January 2027 CNSA 2.0 algorithms will be required in all new NSS acquisitions. NIAP validation is required for commercial products used in NSS and, in the NSA FAQ's words, "will generally require CNSA 2.0 compliance". Where fully certified solutions are not yet available at the end of 2026, the FAQ says a deployment may be accepted on a vendor's contractual commitment to provide a CNSA 2.0 update, combined with a realistic deployment plan that meets the 2030 deadline and is regularly tracked and reported; immediate implementation remains the preferred strategy.

### The Key Takeaway

Organizations can choose to require a Crypto Bill of Materials (CBOM), such as CycloneDX 1.6+, from their vendors — a procurement control, not an external mandate. This standardized inventory supports component visibility and lists the algorithms the supplier records, allowing scoring of vendor readiness and enabling strict contractual deadlines.

### What's Happening

Procurement and GRC teams are tracking overlapping 2026 milestones: NIST IR 8547 (final, October 2026) deprecates classical public-key algorithms after 2030 and disallows them after 2035; US federal civilian agencies follow EO 14412 and OMB M-26-15 (plus a directed FAR contractor rule targeting 31 December 2030 NIST FIPS compliance); NSS follows CNSA 2.0 FAQ v2.2; and EU entities address third-party ICT cryptographic risk under DORA/NIS2 and the EU PQC Roadmap v1.1. Teams are asking for Cryptographic Bills of Materials (CBOMs, e.g., CycloneDX 1.6+) in vendor contracts and verifying active CMVP FIPS 140-3 module certificates rather than accepting ACVP-only listings or marketing claims.
