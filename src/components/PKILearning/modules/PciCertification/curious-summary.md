### What This Is About

A closer look at PCI approval for payment hardware security modules (HSMs), together with the rules for the companies that operate them. It builds on the certification fundamentals module; FIPS 140-3 has its own module.

### Why It Matters

For payment devices, the device approval and the rules for the companies operating it are different things, and both matter. A FIPS 140-3 certificate is not a PCI approval.

### The Key Takeaway

A PCI listing's post-quantum flag only says that support exists — it does not approve a specific algorithm.

### What's Happening

As of 24 September 2026, PCI's newest HSM standard adds post-quantum definitions without naming a required algorithm or deadline.
