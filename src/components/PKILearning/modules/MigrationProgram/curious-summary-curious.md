For an organization with a large, connected technology estate, preparing for quantum-era cryptography usually takes coordinated work across multiple teams and years.

It involves assembling teams from IT, Legal, Finance, and Cyber Security to inventory cryptographic systems, prioritize sensitive data, test new algorithms, and plan deployments across the organization. Clear ownership, risk-based sequencing, testing, and contingency plans can reduce operational disruption and help an organization meet the deadlines that apply to it; they cannot guarantee a flawless migration.
