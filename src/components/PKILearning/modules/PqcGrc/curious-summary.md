### What This Is About

PQC GRC is the governance, risk, and compliance machinery that makes a post-quantum migration measurable and auditable. Where the SOC detects and responds, GRC measures, reports, and governs — turning a multi-year migration into something a board can actually oversee.

### Why It Matters

Quantum risk breaks the standard risk playbook: the timing is uncertain (10 to 30+ years), there are no historical incidents to learn from, and it is at once a technology, compliance, strategic, and operational risk. Logging it as "low likelihood, review annually" is acknowledging the risk, not managing it. The fix is to give quantum risk the same structured treatment as any other top-tier risk.

### The Key Takeaway

Three instruments do most of the work: a Key Risk Indicator cascade that shows the board, the CISO, and operations the right metrics at the right cadence with thresholds that trigger specific action; a risk-appetite statement that lets the program make trade-offs without escalating every one; and a clean GRC-SOC handoff in which the exception register becomes the SOC's suppression list — so genuine cryptographic drift is not lost in alerts from systems that were legitimately deferred.

### What's Happening

Cyber-insurance underwriters are already pricing quantum readiness at renewal, and a documented, governed, measured migration program is a demonstrably better risk than a vague intention to migrate later. The organizations that maintain an evidence dossier can answer the attestation surge after any public quantum event with an export, not a project.
