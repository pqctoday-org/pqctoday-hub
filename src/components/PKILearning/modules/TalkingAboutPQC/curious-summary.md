### What This Is About

You don't need to understand the mathematics to talk about post-quantum cryptography — but you do need to get the facts right. This module is for anyone who has to say something about it at work: in sales, marketing, communications, procurement, policy or the press. It covers what is true today, which deadlines are real and who they apply to, what a security certificate actually proves, and which popular words don't hold up.

### Why It Matters

Customers, buyers and journalists can now check a quantum claim against public records: the standards are published, the government deadlines are written down, and certificate numbers can be looked up. A sentence that overstates a deadline or a certificate costs more trust than it ever wins.

### The Key Takeaway

Say what the record shows, and point to it. Name the standard (for example ML-KEM, FIPS 203), the version of the product, and the evidence — a certificate number or a public roadmap — instead of "quantum-proof" or "fully compliant".

### Try It Yourself

The workshop gives you six real-sounding claims and four customer questions. Pick the most accurate answer and see straight away why it holds up and the others don't.
