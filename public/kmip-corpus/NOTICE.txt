OASIS KMIP test cases - copyright and notice
=============================================

The XML test cases in this folder are copied from OASIS work products of the
OASIS Key Management Interoperability Protocol (KMIP) Technical Committee (the
files under oasis/ are byte-identical to the published package).
They are kept here so the KMIP playground can replay them. manifest.json and
tags-enums.json are PQC Today's own index of these files.

Sources
-------

oasis/mandatory/ and oasis/optional/
  Key Management Interoperability Protocol Profiles Version 3.0, Committee
  Specification Draft 02, 21 May 2026, test cases (test-cases/kmip-v3.0/).
  Edited by Tim Chevalier and Tim Hudson.
  Prose specification: https://docs.oasis-open.org/kmip/kmip-profiles/v3.0/csd02/kmip-profiles-v3.0-csd02.html
  Work product package: https://docs.oasis-open.org/kmip/kmip-profiles/v3.0/csd02/kmip-profiles-v3.0-csd02.zip
  Copyright (c) OASIS Open 2026. All Rights Reserved.

pqc/
  A subset of the OASIS KMIP TC's KMIP 3.0 post-quantum interoperability test
  set (kmip-3-0-pqc-tests-03.zip, 26 February 2025):
  https://groups.oasis-open.org/higherlogic/ws/public/download/72593/kmip-3-0-pqc-tests-03.zip
  Copyright (c) OASIS Open 2025. All Rights Reserved.
