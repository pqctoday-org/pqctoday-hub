    Protecting networks from quantum-era threats


    Quantum computing threatens the long-term security of encrypted data, requiring operators to rethink how they protect sensitive information that must remain secure for years or decades.


    Securing communications in the quantum era demands more than traditional encryption approaches. Networks must protect data in motion against future decryption risks while maintaining confidence that secure services are operating as intended. Adtran addresses this challenge with a defense-in-depth approach that combines multi-layer, post-quantum-ready encryption across optical, Ethernet and IP environments with the capabilities required to operate and validate secure networks at scale.


    In addition to cryptographic protection, Adtran extends security into the physical and operational layers of the network. Infrastructure monitoring and operational insights help teams assess the integrity of the transport environment supporting encrypted traffic, while assured operation supports security-sensitive and mission-critical use cases. Together, these capabilities enable operators to protect long-lived data, validate secure communications and maintain trusted network operation across service provider, enterprise, government and critical infrastructure environments.

    Architecture

    How quantum-safe networks operate

    Quantum safe communications embed encryption across optical, Ethernet and IP layers. Continuous fiber monitoring provides real-time insight to verify integrity and address issues along the transport path, while assured timing supports trusted operations. Together, these protect data, validate infrastructure and enhance operational awareness.
    Learn more

    Operational impact you can measure

    By unifying multi-layer encryption with real-time awareness of the physical transport environment, operators can detect and validate infrastructure issues faster, reducing effort spent confirming faults and addressing security risks. Physical‑layer insights enable early detection of fiber degradation or tampering before they affect secure communications, helping teams determine whether problems stem from the network, encryption systems or underlying infrastructure. Quantum-safe encryption limits exposure of sensitive or long‑lived data to future decryption threats, improving incident response, simplifying compliance validation and reducing uncertainty when assessing potential threats.
    Learn more
