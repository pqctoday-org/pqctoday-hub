## Relevant section — English translation

> Notice soliciting next-generation public-key cryptographic algorithm proposals. ICCS launched the global NGCC algorithm solicitation and released the 'Public-Key Cryptographic Algorithm Submission Requirements (draft)' and 'Public-Key Cryptographic Algorithm Evaluation Criteria (draft)' for global public comment; after revising them per feedback, it now formally accepts public-key algorithm proposals. After the submission deadline (Beijing time), ICCS will conduct a formal review and publicly announce the candidate algorithms entering the first round of evaluation.
