## English translation

> Taiwan released its first official PQC Migration Guide as an interim quantum-safe milestone. Noting that NIST (US) and NCSC (UK) plan to fully retire classical algorithms by 2035, ADI is mentoring seven domestic vendors to develop PQC technology across PKI, defence networks, secure chips, digital signatures, networking equipment and zero-trust identity — an interim step toward Taiwan's quantum-safe transition.
