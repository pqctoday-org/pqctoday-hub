## Relevant section — English translation

> Announcement on launching the Next-Generation Commercial Cryptography (NGCC) algorithm solicitation. To respond to the quantum-computing threat and advance the standardization of next-generation commercial cryptographic algorithms, and in accordance with the work plan of the Cryptography Industry Standardization Technical Committee, the Commercial Cryptography Standards Research Institute (ICCS) will conduct successive global solicitations for next-generation public-key, cryptographic-hash, and block-cipher algorithms — evaluating them on security, performance and characteristics and selecting winning algorithms for standardization. International cooperation in algorithm design is encouraged. (© 2025 ICCS)
