## Original

> 數位發展部數位產業署（數產署）今（16）日攜手「後量子資安產業聯盟（PQC-CIA）」，於臺灣資安大會舉辦「後量子密碼遷移暨晶片應用產業論壇」，正式發表我國首版《後量子密碼遷移指引》。數產署署長林俊秀表示，發布後量子密碼遷移指引…更是資安韌性與國家安全的關鍵布局。聯盟總召集人李維斌指出，將以推動臺灣產業達成「PQC Ready」為目標。

## English translation

> On 16 April 2025, the Ministry of Digital Affairs' Administration for Digital Industries (ADI/數產署), together with the Post-Quantum Cybersecurity Industry Alliance (PQC-CIA), officially released Taiwan's first 'Post-Quantum Cryptography Migration Guide' at the Taiwan Cybersecurity Conference — witnessed by the Financial Supervisory Commission, MODA's cyber-security administration, and the Ministries of Interior/Economic Affairs/Education, Academia Sinica, III and ITRI. ADI Director-General Lin Jun-Xiu called the guide a key pillar of cyber-resilience and national security; the Alliance aims to make Taiwan industry 'PQC Ready'.
