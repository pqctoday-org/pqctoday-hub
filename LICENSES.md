# Third-party licences

The open-source licences of everything the site ships are generated by the
production build and served with it, at
[www.pqctoday.com/licenses/](https://www.pqctoday.com/licenses/) (also linked from the
About page, "Open-source licences"):

- **npm packages**: every package whose code the build puts into the site, and
  the packages whose files are served as they are, each with the licence files
  it ships (`/licenses/npm-packages.txt`).
- **WebAssembly engines**: for each served engine, its third-party contents,
  their licences and the full licence texts (`/licenses/engines/`), including
  the Rust crates read from each binary.

The generator is `scripts/sbom/notices.ts`; its inputs are in
`src/data/licenses/`. The build fails when a shipped package, crate or engine
has no recorded licence.

The Hub's own licence is in [LICENSE](LICENSE). The SBOM on the About page lists
the same components with their versions, and
`public/data/pqctoday-sbom.cdx.json` is the machine-readable form.
